Our Story & Desi Dil

Welcome to Our Cafe (Apna Adda)

Started in 2018 in the cozy bylanes of Indiranagar, Aura Cafe was created by Kabir and a group of college friends who missed having an honest, welcoming neighborhood hangout. No fancy French names, no tiny portions, and no burning a hole in your pocket — just authentic, steaming hot cups of South Indian filter kaapi, slow-brewed adrak-elaichi chai, and comforting bites made fresh everyday.

We source our coffee beans straight from trusted plantations in Chikmagalur and Kodagu (Coorg), roasting them just right for that strong, aromatic morning kick. Our chai is brewed the traditional way with fresh crushed ginger, fragrant green cardamom, and rich boiled milk.

Pair your drink with our famous pillowy-soft Irani bun maska, crispy golden samosas, and loaded Bombay grilled sandwiches. Whether you're working remotely on your laptop, sharing college gossip with friends, or having a peaceful Sunday breakfast with family, Aura Cafe is always your home away from home.
